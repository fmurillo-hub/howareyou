What each label means

Handle with care. These labels are rough shortcuts, and people use some of them as insults. The core difference between capitalism and socialism or communism is who owns the means of production (factories, land, big firms): private owners, or society as a whole. Ten questions cannot measure that fully, and none of them asks about political freedoms such as elections, free speech or one-party rule, which also matter a lot for labels like "communist". No country fits one label purely: most real economies mix private and public ownership.
